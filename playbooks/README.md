## How to Use

1. Pick one vertical to focus on for 90 days
2. Download the playbook for that vertical
3. Follow the sales scripts, keywords, and tech stack guides
4. Offer the free assessment (HIPAA, compliance, or tech audit)
5. Close with the package pricing

## Lead Generation Ideas

- **Local meetups:** Attend dental association meetings, realtor board meetings
- **Google Ads:** Target vertical-specific keywords (see each playbook)
- **Direct mail:** Send HIPAA/compliance checklist to 100 dental offices
- **Referrals:** Offer 1 month free for any referral that signs
- **Partnerships:** Partner with dental supply reps, real estate broker-owners
